LuciBet does not set cookies and does not use advertising or analytics trackers. We use your browser's local storage (similar technology) for the items below. Strictly necessary items work without consent; the optional one only runs if you choose "Accept all" in the banner.

Third parties

We load Chart.js from cdn.jsdelivr.net and GSAP from cdnjs.cloudflare.com so the site works. Those hosts can see your IP address when your browser requests the files. We do not load fonts from Google, and we do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or similar tools.

Your choices

Use "Cookie settings" in the footer to change your choice at any time. Choosing "Essential only" stops the live-visitor counter. You can also clear this storage in your browser settings; you will then be logged out.
