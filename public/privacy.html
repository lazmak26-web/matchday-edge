3. Payments

Payments are arranged manually through Telegram. We do not receive or store card numbers on this website. Telegram and your payment provider handle your payment data under their own policies.

4. Who else receives data

  • Email delivery (Resend): sends your one-time login code to your email address.
  • KeyAuth (keyauth.win): only if you log in with a licence key rather than an email account.
  • Hosting provider: {{HOSTING_PROVIDER}}, which processes requests (including IP addresses) to serve the site.
  • Script hosts: your browser loads Chart.js from cdn.jsdelivr.net and GSAP from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. Those hosts see your IP address and browser type when you load the page. Team badge images are loaded from the sports-data providers' servers.
  • Sports-data providers (football-data.org and a second odds/fixtures provider): our server queries them for match data. No personal data about you is sent to them.
  • Telegram: if you contact us or pay through Telegram, Telegram processes that conversation.

Some of these providers may be located outside your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards.

5. Storage in your browser

We use your browser's local storage for login, language and preferences. Details are in the Cookie Policy.

6. How long we keep data

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Login codes expire after 10 minutes and sessions after 24 hours. When you delete your account we remove your email, password hash and plan data and unlink any licence from your email. We keep only a one-way hash of a used licence key so it cannot be activated again; it cannot identify you. We may keep limited records where the law requires it.

7. Your rights

  • Delete your data: sign in, open Settings and choose "Delete my account", or contact us. Licence-key (KeyAuth) accounts are deleted on request through Telegram or email.
  • Access, correct or receive a copy of your data, and object to or restrict processing: contact us.
  • Withdraw consent at any time through "Cookie settings" in the footer. This does not affect earlier processing.
  • Complain to your data-protection authority (for example the CNDP in Morocco under Law 09-08, or your national authority in the EU/EEA).

We answer requests within 30 days.

8. Age limit

LuciBet is for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt, connections use HTTPS, sessions expire, and login attempts are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at once if you suspect a problem.

10. Emails

We only send transactional emails you ask for, such as a login code. We do not send marketing emails or newsletters, so there is nothing to unsubscribe from. If that ever changes we will ask for your consent first and include an unsubscribe link in every message.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
